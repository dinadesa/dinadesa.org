Dinadesa is a 501(c)3 non-profit dedicated to improving public schools through open-source software focused on administrative processes.

It was created by entrepreneur David Binetti, who also serves as a Trustee for the West Ada School District.

The Dinadesa name is a portmanteau of his daughter's nicknames (which is the best we could come up for the time being -- we'll see if it sticks...)
